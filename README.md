
# Europe Bike Sales Analysis

## Project Overview
This project explores bike sales data to uncover business insights into sales performance, customer purchasing behaviour, product demand, and revenue trends across European markets.

The goal is to transform raw sales data into meaningful insights that can support data-driven business decisions.

## Business Objectives
- Evaluate overall bike sales performance.
- Identify top-performing products and product categories.
- Analyze sales trends across available European countries and regions.
- Understand customer purchasing patterns.
- Identify opportunities to improve sales performance and profitability.

## Tools and Technologies
- Microsoft Excel — data cleaning, analysis, and reporting.
- Data visualization — charts and dashboards, where applicable.

## Project Workflow
1. Data inspection and understanding.
2. Data cleaning and preparation.
3. Exploratory data analysis.
4. KPI development and visualization.
5. Business insights and recommendations.

## Key Performance Indicators
The analysis may include the following metrics, depending on the available dataset:
- Total revenue
- Total units sold
- Sales by country
- Sales by product category
- Sales trends over time
- Customer segment performance
